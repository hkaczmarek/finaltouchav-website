Lifestyle

A Day in the Life

From the moment you wake up to the moment you fall asleep, your home works with you — not against you.

7:00 AM — Good Morning
Your day begins effortlessly

Shades rise, thermostat wakes to 70°, a morning playlist starts softly, coffee maker turns on — all triggered by one tap or schedule.

Noon — Work from Home
Focused and connected

Office lights shift to focused white light. Doorbell camera notifies your phone. All cameras accessible from your desk in one app.

6:30 PM — Movie Night
One tap. Cinema mode.

Lights dim to amber, projector powers on, Dolby Atmos kicks in, phone calls go to silent, HVAC adjusts — all from one button.

10:00 PM — Good Night
Everything powers down

All lights off, doors lock, alarm arms, thermostat drops to sleep temp, TVs and devices power down. One scene, total peace of mind.
