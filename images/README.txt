FINAL TOUCH AV - Website Images
================================

Replace these files with your own photos.
Keep the exact filenames — the website references them by name.
JPG format recommended. No need to resize — CSS handles cropping.

TIPS:
-----
- Photos from your actual projects will always look better than stock
- Landscape orientation works best for service cards (they crop to 160px tall)
- The about image works best in portrait or square orientation
- Dark or moody photos match the site aesthetic well
